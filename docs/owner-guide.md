# Your website: owner guide

**Website:** [[yourdomain.co.uk]]
**For changes, contact:** [[your name, business email and phone]]

## What you own
- **Your domain** ([[yourdomain.co.uk]]), registered in your name at [[registrar]]. It renews on **[[date]]**.
- **Your Google Business Profile.**
- **Your website content:** your words, prices and photos.

## The one thing you must not miss
**Keep your domain renewed.** Leave auto-renew on and keep the card up to date at [[registrar]]. If it lapses, your website and any email on that address stop working.

## Asking for a change
Send me what you'd like changed, in plain words, for example:
- "The full clean is now £60."
- "We're closed 24 to 27 December."
- "Please add this review from Sam." (Check they're happy to be named.)
- "Here are three new photos."

Included in your plan: [[what's included]]. I normally reply within [[2 working days]], and within [[1 working day]] if the site is down or a phone number is wrong.

## Keep these up to date
Tell me when any of these change, so your site never misleads customers:
- Prices and services
- Opening hours and holiday closures
- Phone number, email or booking link
- Areas you cover, or your address
- Policies, such as cancellation

## Getting more customers from your site
- **Ask happy customers for a Google review.** Your review link: [[link]]
- **Keep your Google Business Profile current:** hours, photos and services.
- **Share your website link** on social media, your van, cards and invoices.

## Security
- Use a strong password and two-step login on your domain and Google accounts.
- **Never share your passwords**, including with me.
- Be wary of emails saying your domain is expiring or offering "SEO". Check by logging in to [[registrar]] directly, not through the email's link.

## If you want to leave, or I'm unavailable
Your site keeps running on its own. If you ever want to move, or I stop offering this service, you'll get a copy of your website files and your domain settings, free, within **14 days** of asking. Your domain is already yours.

## Something's wrong?
1. Check it on another device or on mobile data.
2. Send me a screenshot and the page address.
