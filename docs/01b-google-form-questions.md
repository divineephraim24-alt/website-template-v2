# Google Form version of the intake questions

**Set this up once** at forms.google.com, signed in to your **business** Google account, then reuse the link for every client.

Settings: turn on "Limit to 1 response" only if you want clients to sign in. Leave "Collect email addresses" on so you know who replied. Mark only the questions with * as required. Don't use the form to collect passwords, and tell clients never to send them.

Form title: **Your new website: a few questions**

Form description (paste):
> Answer what you can. "Not sure" is fine, and we can go through the rest together. It takes about 20 minutes. Please don't enter any passwords.

Types: **S** short answer, **P** paragraph, **M** multiple choice, **C** checkboxes.

## Section 1: Your business
- * Business name, exactly as it should appear (S)
- * In one line, what do you do and where? (S)
- * Type of business (M): Trade / Cleaning / Beauty / Food / Fitness / Mobile service / Shop / Other
- * Should the site say (M): "I" (just me) / "we" (a team)
- * Business structure (M): Sole trader / Limited company / Not sure
- If limited: company name, number, where registered, registered office address (P)
- Payment methods you accept (S)

## Section 2: How customers reach you
- * How should customers contact you? (C): WhatsApp / Phone / Email / Online booking link
- * Which is the main one? (M): WhatsApp / Phone / Email / Online booking link
- Your WhatsApp and phone numbers (S)
- Your email address (S)
- Your booking link (S)
- Instagram, Facebook and TikTok links (P)
- Google Business Profile link, if you have one (S)

## Section 3: Where you work
- * Where do you work? (M): I go to customers / Customers come to me / Both
- If you travel: towns and areas covered, and how far you'll go (P)
- If you travel: what does the customer need to provide? (P)
- If customers visit: full address and Google Maps link (P)
- If customers visit: opening hours for each day, and holiday closures (P)
- If customers visit: walk-ins or appointment only? Parking? (P)

## Section 4: What you sell
- * Your services or packages and what's included (P)
- * Prices: fixed, "from", or by quote (P)
- Do prices change by size or option? Paste your price list (P)
- Add-ons or extras, with prices (P)
- Regular visits, memberships or subscriptions: how they work and cost (P)

## Section 5: How it works
- * How does a customer book, step by step? (P)
- Cancellation, deposit and late policies (P)
- What do customers ask most often, and what do you tell them? (P)
- Anything customers should know before booking, or limits on what you can do (P)
- How do you handle customers' personal details? (P)
- Your terms: how bookings are confirmed, how long quotes last, when and how customers pay (P)
- If a customer isn't happy, what do you do, and when do you give money back? (P)

## Section 6: Why choose you
- Two to four genuine reasons to choose you (P)
- Insurance, qualifications or memberships you'd like shown (P)
- Customer reviews, word for word, with first name and town (P)
- Has each of those customers agreed to being shown? (M): Yes, all / Some / Not yet / No reviews yet

## Section 7: Look and photos
- Do you have a logo? (M): Yes / No
- Brand colours, if any (S)
- Preferred look (M): Dark / Light / No preference
- Style (M): Modern / Classic / Friendly / No preference
- Corners (M): Sharp / Soft / Round / No preference
- Websites you like the look of (P)
- Photo instructions: add this as a **text block**, with a link to your shared copy of `client-photo-guide.md`:
  > Please send your best photos and any short videos as originals (not screenshots). Please read the short photo guide first.
- Do you own these photos, or have permission to use them? (M): Yes / Some / Not sure

## Section 8: Web address
- Do you already own a web address? (M): Yes / No / Not sure
- If yes: what is it, and who is it with? (S)
- If no: what would you like it to be? (S)
- Do you use email on that address? (M): Yes / No / Not sure
- Do you have an existing website? Link (S)

## Section 9: The project
- When would you like it live? (S)
- Who will approve the wording? (S)
- After launch, would you like (M): Ongoing updates (care plan) / One-off build / Not sure
- Anything else? (P)

**Photos:** don't use the form's file upload, which needs a Google sign-in. Ask for photos by shared Drive folder, WeTransfer or similar, so they arrive as originals. WhatsApp compresses photos unless sent "as a document".
