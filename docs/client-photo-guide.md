# Taking photos for your website

Good, honest photos of your real work do more for your website than anything else. A recent phone is fine.

## Taking them
- **Hold the phone sideways (landscape)** for most photos. Take a few upright too, for phone screens.
- **Use good light.** Daylight is best. Avoid harsh midday sun, dark rooms and strong light behind the subject.
- **Tidy the background.** Move bins, tools, bags and clutter out of shot.
- **Wipe the lens** with a soft cloth first. It makes a surprising difference.
- **Hold steady and tap the screen** on the main subject to focus.
- **Take more than you need,** from a few angles. We'll pick the best.

## Before and after
- Take both from the **same spot and the same angle**, with similar light.
- Take the "before" **before you touch anything**. It's easy to forget.

## What to photograph
- Your finished work, close up and from further back
- You or your team at work (only with everyone's agreement)
- Your van, shop front, or the inside of your premises
- The details that show quality

## Keep people's privacy
Avoid these where you can, and tell us if any are in a photo so they can be blurred:
- **Number plates**
- **House numbers** and street names
- **People's faces**, unless they've agreed to be on your website
- Children, always, unless a parent has agreed in writing
- Letters, screens, or anything with personal details

## Please only send
- Photos **you took or have permission to use**. Don't send images from Google or other people's social media.

## Sending them
- Send the **originals**, not screenshots.
- Use a **shared Google Drive folder or WeTransfer**. If you use WhatsApp, send them **"as a document"**, or they lose quality.
- Short videos are welcome: a few seconds each, filmed sideways or upright, held steady.
