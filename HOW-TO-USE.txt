 1. WHAT THIS IS
===============================================================================

This toolkit lets you build fast, professional, phone-first websites for any
small business: trades, cleaners, beauty, food, fitness, mobile services and
shops.

You don't need to write code. You give Claude the client's details, Claude
builds the site one section at a time, and you approve each step.

How a website gets made:

  Client's information
     goes into one file (content.js)
     the build tool checks it and makes the website (the site folder)
     the check tool tests it
     it is uploaded to a private GitHub repo
     Cloudflare publishes it
     it appears on the client's own web address

There is no demo site in the toolkit. Use a real site you've already built
when you want to show someone an example.

INSIDE A CLIENT'S FOLDER

  client\content.js   Every fact about the business. The most important file.
  client\media\       The client's original photos, videos and logo.
                      These are never edited.
  site\               The finished website. This is what gets published.
  QUESTIONS.md        What you still need to ask the client.
  PLAN.md             The record for this client: progress, sign-off,
                      accounts and decisions.
  docs\               A copy of the guides.
  engine\, tools\     The working parts. You can ignore these.
  .tmp\               Temporary files, including the preview file.

IMPORTANT: client folders are saved on this computer only. They are NOT in
OneDrive and are NOT backed up to GitHub, apart from the published site. Keep
your own backup of client folders, especially the original photos.

 3. DO THESE ONCE, BEFORE YOUR FIRST CLIENT
===============================================================================

[ ] CLOUDFLARE SECURITY (essential)
    Every client's site will sit in your Cloudflare account.
      - Give it its own strong password, not only "Continue with GitHub"
      - Turn on two-factor authentication
      - Store the recovery codes safely, away from this computer

[ ] GITHUB SECURITY
    Turn on two-factor authentication and store the recovery codes safely.

[ ] BUSINESS EMAIL
    Create a separate email for client messages and client-related accounts.
    Keep it apart from your personal email and any other business.

[ ] GOOGLE FORM
    Sign in to your business Google account, go to forms.google.com, and build
    the intake form from docs\01b-google-form-questions.md. Keep the link. You
    reuse the same form for every client.

[ ] PHOTO GUIDE
    Put a copy of docs\client-photo-guide.md somewhere you can share by link,
    such as Google Docs, and add that link to your form.

[ ] BUSINESS BASICS (read business\notes.md)
      - Check whether you need to tell HMRC about this income
      - Ask a broker about professional indemnity insurance
      - Check whether you need to pay the ICO data protection fee
      - Decide your prices and what they include

[ ] YOUR SALES SHEET AND PROPOSAL
    Fill in your details and prices in business\what-you-get.md and
    business\proposal-template.md. Anything in [[double brackets]] needs
    replacing.

 5. THE FULL PROCESS, STEP BY STEP
===============================================================================

Text between the lines marked PASTE is ready to copy into Claude Code. Replace
anything in [square brackets].

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 0: TOOLKIT HEALTH CHECK (before each new client)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Open C:\Users\HP\Projects\Website-Template in VS Code, then open Claude Code.

--- PASTE ---
Run a toolkit health check. Build temporary test content in .tmp/tests for 3
made-up businesses (mobile; premises in light mode; both, with video and an
extra page) using different contact methods, sections and style options. Run
build_site and check_site on each, and confirm broken content is refused.
Check docs/04-hosting-domain-ownership.md against Cloudflare's current
documentation and tell me anything that has changed. Then delete all test
content, confirm git status is clean, and report the results.
--- END ---

Carry on only if it reports no problems.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: COLLECT THE CLIENT'S INFORMATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Send your Google Form link and the photo guide, or go through
docs\01-intake-questions.md on a call.

You need:
  - What they do, and their services and prices
  - How customers should contact them
  - Areas covered, or address and opening hours
  - Booking steps and policies
  - Photos and logo
  - Real reviews, with each customer's permission
  - Sole trader or limited company
  - Whether they own a domain, and whether they use email on it

Don't worry if some of it is missing. Claude will list the gaps.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: QUOTE AND DEPOSIT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Fill in business\proposal-template.md and send it. Don't start building until
they've accepted and paid the deposit.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: START THE CLIENT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

With Claude Code open in the toolkit folder, type:

    /new-client Client Name

Then paste this, filled in:

--- PASTE ---
Business type: [what they do]
Service mode: [we go to them / they come to us / both]
Here is everything I have: [paste notes, form answers, prices, policies]
Photos, logo and videos are in: [folder path, or "none yet"]
Fill in content.js from this only. Don't invent anything. List everything
missing in QUESTIONS.md as plain questions, suggest which sections suit this
business with one-line reasons, then stop and wait for my OK.
--- END ---

Claude will create the folder, fill in what it can, write the questions,
suggest sections, and stop.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: FILL THE GAPS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Send the questions in QUESTIONS.md to the client. When they reply:

--- PASTE ---
Here are the client's answers: [paste]. Update content.js and QUESTIONS.md.
Show me what's still missing.
--- END ---

Then agree the sections and look:

--- PASTE ---
Use these sections: [list, or "your suggestions are fine"].
Style: [dark or light, accent colour, modern/classic/friendly font,
sharp/soft/round corners].
--- END ---

Then sort out the photos:

--- PASTE ---
Copy the photos from [folder] into client/media without changing the
originals. Show me a contact sheet and suggest which photo goes where. Check
each one for number plates, house numbers, faces and signs, add blur boxes,
and show me zoomed crops to confirm.
--- END ---

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: BUILD
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--- PASTE ---
Build the site one section at a time, in page order. After each, show phone
and desktop screenshots and wait for my OK.
--- END ---

Reply "OK, next", or say what you'd like changed.

Until the client approves the wording, every page shows a yellow DRAFT WORDING
banner and the site is hidden from Google. That's deliberate.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6: CLIENT APPROVAL
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Say:

--- PASTE ---
Make the preview file.
--- END ---

You get one .html file in the client's .tmp folder. Send it to the client,
with a message like:

    Here's a preview of your website. Please check every price, time, detail
    and sentence. Reply with any changes, or "I approve the wording" when
    you're happy.

If the file is over 16 MB, send it by Google Drive instead of WhatsApp.

When they approve in writing:

--- PASTE ---
The client approved the wording: [who, how, date]. Record it in PLAN.md and
content.js, and set wordingDraft to false.
--- END ---

Keep their approval message. The site can't be built for launch without this.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7: PRE-LAUNCH CHECK
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Open the CLIENT'S folder in VS Code, open Claude Code, and type:

    /prelaunch-check

Fix anything it finds. Then work through the by-hand items in
docs\03-prelaunch-checklist.md, including tapping every contact button on your
own phone. Test on an iPhone if the site has a video.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 8: GO LIVE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--- PASTE ---
Walk me through going live, one step at a time, using
docs/04-hosting-domain-ownership.md. Show me each dry run before anything is
uploaded. Don't touch the domain or DNS until I say so.
--- END ---

Before the domain is changed, always check:
  - The domain is in the client's name and paid by them
  - Their existing MX records (email) are written down
  - DNSSEC is off

Changing the domain wrongly can stop a client's email, so take this slowly.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 9: HAND OVER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--- PASTE ---
Fill in docs/owner-guide.md for this client and list the accounts and renewal
dates for the handover.
--- END ---

Then:
  - Send the client the owner guide
  - Confirm what they own
  - Add an uptime monitor
  - Set up the care plan, if agreed
  - Invoice the balance


===============================================================================
 6. THE TWO COMMANDS
===============================================================================

/new-client Client Name
  Use in the TOOLKIT folder. Creates the client's folder, fills in content
  from your notes, lists questions, suggests sections, then waits. It refuses
  to touch a folder that already exists.

/prelaunch-check
  Use in the CLIENT'S folder. Checks sign-off, runs all the tests, compares
  facts with your notes, inspects photos and reports problems by priority. It
  doesn't change or publish anything.

 7. WHAT A WEBSITE CAN INCLUDE
===============================================================================

Everything except the top section (the "hero") is optional.

SECTIONS
  Hero                 Main headline, button and photo
  Reassurance          Two to four short reasons to trust the business
  Services             Cards with prices, "from" prices or "free quote"
  Price table          Prices by size or option
  Add-ons              Optional extras
  Plan                 Regular visits, memberships or subscriptions
  Our work             Video, before and after photos, gallery
  How booking works    Simple steps and policies
  Reviews              Real reviews, with consent
  Where we work        Areas covered and what the customer provides
  Find us              Address, map link, opening hours, walk-ins, parking
  Closing              Final call to action
  FAQ page             Questions, answers and a privacy note
  Policies             Privacy, terms and conditions, and refunds and
                       cancellations, linked at the bottom of every page
  Extra pages          A menu, service page, price list or gallery

CONTACT METHODS (any mix, with one as the main button)
  WhatsApp, phone, email, online booking link

TYPE OF BUSINESS
  mobile     We come to you
  premises   Visit us
  both       Either

STYLE
  Mode       dark or light
  Accent     Any colour. If it's hard to read, the build suggests a fix.
  Font       modern, classic or friendly
  Corners    sharp, soft or round
  Logo       Optional. Favicons and the link preview image are made for you.

BUILT IN AUTOMATICALLY
  Security settings, Google business details, sitemap, 404 page, link preview
  image, iPhone video fix, keyboard and screen-reader support, reduced-motion
  support. No cookies or tracking.

CHECKED AUTOMATICALLY BEFORE LAUNCH
  No passwords or API keys in the published files, no forms without spam
  protection, no tracking without a cookie banner, policy pages linked, and
  robots.txt letting Google in.


===============================================================================
 8. THE CONTENT FILE (content.js)
===============================================================================

Normally Claude edits this for you. If you open it yourself:

  - Only change text inside quote marks, or true/false switches
  - Keep the comma at the end of each line
  - [[Double brackets]] are placeholders that still need filling in
  - Switch a section off by changing true to false under "sections"
  - If text contains an apostrophe, wrap it in "double quotes"
  - Prices are plain numbers with no quotes or £ sign, like 45. If there is
    no fixed price, set price to null and put the words in priceText
  - Terms and refund wording comes only from the client's own answers

The build won't run while something is wrong. It tells you what to fix and
usually the line number, and it leaves the existing website untouched.


===============================================================================
 9. PHOTOS AND VIDEOS
===============================================================================

PHOTOS
  - Originals in client\media are never changed
  - The tools make web copies, turn them upright, remove hidden data such as
    GPS location, and make phone and desktop sizes
  - Blur boxes cover number plates and house numbers. Claude adds them, and
    you should always check zoomed-in crops yourself
  - Only use photos the client owns or has permission to use

VIDEOS
  - The toolkit doesn't edit or compress video yet
  - Use a short .mp4 that's already compressed
  - Aim for under 15 MB. The build refuses anything over 25 MB
  - Check the video yourself for plates, house numbers and faces. The photo
    blur tool does NOT work on video

 10. CHANGING A CLIENT'S WEBSITE LATER
===============================================================================

Open the CLIENT'S folder in VS Code, open Claude Code, and say:

--- PASTE ---
Read PLAN.md. [Describe the change.] Update content.js, rebuild, check it, and
show me before publishing.
--- END ---

Examples:
  - The full clean is now £60.
  - We're closed 24 to 27 December.
  - Add this review from Sam in [town]. They've agreed to be named.
  - Replace the main photo with the new one in client/media/photos.

When you're happy, say "publish". Claude should build with --live, run the
check, do a dry run, then commit and push from the site folder. Cloudflare
updates the site about a minute later.


===============================================================================
 11. UNDOING A CHANGE
===============================================================================

IF THE LIVE SITE LOOKS WRONG RIGHT NOW
  Cloudflare > Workers & Pages > the client's project > Deployments.
  Find the last good version, open its "..." menu, and choose Rollback.
  It switches back within seconds.

  Then tell Claude what you rolled back, so the files can be corrected.
  Otherwise the next publish brings the mistake back.

TO UNDO IT PROPERLY
  Say: "Undo the last change to the website." Claude should keep the history
  and never force-push.

 12. HOSTING, DOMAINS AND WHO OWNS WHAT
===============================================================================

STANDARD SETUP
  Domain                     Client owns it and pays for it
  Google Business Profile    Client owns it
  Website files              Client's once paid in full
  Hosting                    Your Cloudflare account, free tier
  Code                       Your private GitHub

YOUR FOUR PROTECTION RULES
  1. The domain is always in the client's name and paid by them.
  2. Exit plan: on request, they get a zip of the site and a DNS export, free,
     within 14 days.
  3. Never add or change email records you haven't agreed to manage.
  4. A client who insists can have the site in their own Cloudflare account.

IF THE CLIENT HAS NO DOMAIN
  The recommendation is a .co.uk, bought by the client on their own account.
  Namecheap is suggested, but any reputable registrar is fine. Use their email
  and card, turn on auto-renew, and skip the upsells. Check the renewal price.

IF YOU'RE UNAVAILABLE
  The site keeps running without you. Your response times, holiday cover and
  what happens if you stop are in the proposal and in docs\04. Fill in your
  own terms before sending a proposal.

Full instructions: docs\04-hosting-domain-ownership.md

 13. MONEY, CONTRACTS AND STAYING LEGAL
===============================================================================

This is not legal or tax advice. Check gov.uk, HMRC and the ICO, and speak to
an accountant or solicitor if you're unsure.

  - Take a deposit before starting
  - Keep every approval in writing
  - Limit revision rounds (two is a sensible start)
  - Don't publish until the balance is paid, unless you've agreed otherwise
  - Never promise Google rankings or sales
  - Never ask for a client's passwords
  - Track your hours on the first few jobs, then adjust your prices
  - Have the proposal checked before relying on it as a contract

Pricing ideas and a contract checklist are in business\notes.md.


===============================================================================
 14. THE RULES THAT ALWAYS APPLY
===============================================================================

  - Never invent facts: prices, hours, reviews, qualifications or claims
  - Reviews are real, word for word, and shown with permission
  - The client approves all wording in writing before launch
  - Never edit the client's original files
  - Blur number plates and house numbers
  - Check before publishing
  - Do a dry run before every git save or upload
  - Only the site folder goes to the client's GitHub repo
  - Never put passwords or recovery codes in a project file
  - Never put one client's details in another client's site

 15. WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG
===============================================================================

"The build stopped"
  Read the numbered list. Nothing has been changed. Paste it to Claude and
  say "fix these", or fill in what's missing.

"placeholder(s) still need filling in"
  Fill them in, or switch that section off.

"This is a live build but the wording is still marked as draft"
  Get the client's written approval and record it (Step 6).

"The accent colour is too hard to read"
  Use the suggested colour in the message.

"Reviews are on but consentConfirmed is not true"
  Get each customer's permission first, then tell Claude.

"FFmpeg was not found"
  Run: winget install Gyan.FFmpeg

"No built site found"
  Build it first: node tools/build_site.js .

The check reports problems
  Paste the result to Claude and ask it to explain and propose a fix.

Can't find a Cloudflare setting
  Its menus move. Press Ctrl+K in the dashboard and search for the name.

The domain still shows the old site
  This can take a few hours after a nameserver change. Try mobile data.

Client's email stopped working
  Stop and compare the DNS records with the list you wrote down before
  changing anything. Restore any missing MX or TXT records.

You've forgotten where you got to
  Open the client's folder and say: "Read PLAN.md and QUESTIONS.md and tell me
  where this project is and what to do next."

 17. WHAT THE TOOLKIT DOES NOT DO YET
===============================================================================

STILL TO PROVE ON A REAL JOB
  The toolkit passed its tests with made-up businesses and generated images.
  On your first client, allow extra time and check these carefully:
    - Publishing through Cloudflare
    - Video on a real iPhone
    - Real photos and blur placement
    - The full /new-client conversation

NOT BUILT (the Version 2 list in README.md)
  - Time log for each client
  - More themes and a theme preview
  - Video editing and compression tool
  - Extra commands (/review-section, /publish, /update-content)
  - Full handover pack
  - Testing on your phone over home Wi-Fi
  - An automated self-test

The toolkit also doesn't do online shops, payments, customer logins, contact
forms, blogs or email setup.

IMPROVING THE TOOLKIT
  Each client gets their own copy of the tools. If you fix something during a
  job and want it for future clients, ask Claude to make the same fix in the
  main toolkit folder and back it up to GitHub. Existing client sites don't
  update on their own.
